## Greenfield Governance — Clarify Addendum

Apply this addendum in addition to the core command. Classify each material
clarification by the authority required for its answer, not by the command in
which it was raised:

- Product-significant scope, capability, actor, authority, ownership, or
  boundary answers require Canonical PRD approval.
- Material architecture answers require Controlled Architecture Change to the
  approved `architecture/baseline.md`.
- Feature decomposition, boundary, dependency, or ordering answers require a
  `ROADMAP.md` update.
- Only Feature-local answers may be written directly to Feature artifacts.

Stop for the owning authority before relying on an upstream answer. Do not use
a leading or implementation-convenient question to manufacture permission for
an upstream change.
